Why harmonize? The raw records do not agree

Monthly totals, all years

VIIRS sees smaller fires and reports several pixels per fire, so its raw counts jump when it starts. The harmonized line counts 5 km burning cells and rescales VIIRS onto the MODIS scale, so it stays continuous across both sensors.
