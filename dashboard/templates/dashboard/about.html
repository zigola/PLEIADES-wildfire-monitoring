ABOUT PLEIADES

From satellite pixels to fire intelligence.

Burning activity, from wildfires to agricultural burns, follows patterns across seasons and regions. PLEIADES makes those patterns visible.

The challenge

Satellites have tracked active-fire hotspots for more than two decades, but the record is split across sensors whose data cannot be compared directly. Fire managers are left without a consistent picture of when and where burning has occurred.

PLEIADES harmonizes those records into a single burning activity calendar, so emergency responders, scientists and land managers can examine historical fire patterns, unusual conditions and critical periods, and use them for early warning.

What the platform does

  • Combines MODIS and VIIRS active-fire detections into one comparable record.
  • Shows when burning is typical, and when it is unusual.
  • Offers an AI assistant for questions about recent fires, weather and risk.
  • Connects IoT monitoring sites and routes alerts to the people responsible, with human confirmation before wider warnings.
