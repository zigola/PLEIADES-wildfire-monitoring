How to read these results

  • Satellite thermal anomaly: a pixel flagged as hot by MODIS or VIIRS. It is not, by itself, a confirmed wildfire (it can be a gas flare, industrial heat or a false alarm).
  • Verified NASA observation: a record published by NASA FIRMS and stored unchanged in PLEIADES with its source product.
  • Historical observation: a NASA record from an earlier date. It is never shown as current.
  • Statistical estimate: an expectation computed from earlier years. It is not an observation, it has no coordinates and it never says that a fire exists.
