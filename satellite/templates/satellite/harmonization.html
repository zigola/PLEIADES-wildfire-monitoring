    Harmonization methodology

    1. Validate and normalizeEvery FIRMS row is checked (coordinates, UTC date and time, FRP sign, numeric fields) and mapped to one schema. Rejected rows are counted per reason in the import history, never silently dropped.
    2. Preserve measurementsMODIS brightness (T21, T31) and VIIRS brightness (I-4, I-5) keep separate meanings; the original confidence string, satellite code, product, version and import are stored on every row.
    3. Exact duplicatesSame sensor, platform, latitude, longitude, UTC date and time in different products (typically NRT and SP) are linked; the SP copy stays active. Nothing is deleted.
    4. Overlap groupsA MODIS and a VIIRS detection are linked when their pixel footprints overlap (centre distance at most the mean pixel size from the record's scan and track) within a heuristic time window (180 minutes by default, adjustable from 10 to 720; neither value is a validated threshold). Groups are connected components of such links. Both detections stay in the data; this is a flag for analysis, not a merge.
    5. Comparable activityFor calendars, detections are snapped to 5 km cells and counted as active cell-days; VIIRS is rescaled to MODIS with a factor estimated on the overlap period of the selected area. This is an index of activity, not hectares burned.

    Limitations: overlap thresholds are heuristic; confidence classes of the two sensors are not equivalent; cloud, overpass timing and scan geometry change what each sensor can see.
