Understand the system

Ask AI about these readings →
Sensor health

A field is reported only when a packet contains a value. A recent packet does not prove physical sensor calibration or accuracy.

LoRa connectivity

RSSI and SNR describe received radio-link quality. Packet age determines ONLINE/OFFLINE status; missing radio metrics are unknown.

Solar & battery

Panel voltage/current/power and battery voltage are shown only if the device sends them. Voltage alone cannot prove charging health.

Fire screening

Temperature, gas and smoke indicators are screening signals. Validate thresholds against the actual hardware and local conditions before deployment.
